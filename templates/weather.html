Balcony Heat Shielding

Terra cotta and dark plastic pots absorb extreme heat in direct sun. When ambient temperatures exceed 32°C, mulch the surface with coco peat or wood chips to retain moisture.

High-Floor Wind Evaporation

Breezes on balconies above the 3rd floor can double leaf transpiration rates. Group containers together to create a humid microclimate.

Bottom Watering Technique

For herbs and tomatoes, pour water into the saucer and let the potting soil absorb from below for 20 minutes, then drain excess. This keeps leaves dry and prevents fungal blights!
